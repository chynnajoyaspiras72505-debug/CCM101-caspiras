# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

Completing this activity helped me understand the differences and similarities among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Among the three, AWS impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and mature cloud ecosystem. I learned that it can support many types of workloads, from simple virtual machines to large global applications.

The three cloud platforms have many similarities. They all provide computing, storage, networking, identity management, databases, and managed Kubernetes services. For example, Amazon EC2, Azure Virtual Machines, and Google Compute Engine all provide virtual machines. However, each provider also has different strengths. AWS has a broad service portfolio, Azure integrates strongly with Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud is especially strong in data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and Kubernetes.

I also learned that business requirements should influence cloud platform selection. A company should not choose a provider only because it is popular. Its existing technologies, budget, scalability requirements, application type, global reach, and technical needs should be considered. For example, an organization already using many Microsoft products may benefit from Azure, while an AI research company may benefit from Google Cloud.

This activity also helped me understand the role of a Cloud Solutions Consultant. The role requires comparing technologies and matching technical services with actual business requirements. A good recommendation should explain not only which platform to use, but also why its services are appropriate for the client.

The most challenging part of this mission was comparing similar services across the three providers because their names and features are different. By completing the comparison tables, client scenarios, and Linux investigation, I became more comfortable identifying equivalent services and understanding how a Linux server could be migrated to AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
